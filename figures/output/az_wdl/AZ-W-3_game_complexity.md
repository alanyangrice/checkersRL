Dual-axis plot (x = epochs 1–250): left y-axis shows average moves per self-play game (5-epoch centered rolling mean with raw trace); right y-axis shows epoch wall-clock time (minutes, dash-dot); dashed verticals mark Phase 2 (epoch 16) and Phase 3 (epoch 66). Phase transitions drive massive immediate jumps due to curriculum shifts and MCTS budget changes (75→200→400 sims/move): Phase 1→2 (epoch 15→16) increases avg_moves 52.3→77.1 and epoch time 1.5→4.6 min (~~3.1×); Phase 2→3 (epoch 65→66) increases avg_moves 70.6→112.1 and epoch time 6.4→17.1 min (~~2.7×). However, Phase 3 is not flat: game length drifts upward to peak at 141.5 moves (epoch 156), driving epoch time to a maximum of 22.7 min (epoch 213), before relaxing to 114.4 moves and 16.7 min by epoch 250. The 500k-position replay buffer saturates at epoch 91.
