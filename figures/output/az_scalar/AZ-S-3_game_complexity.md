Dual-axis plot (epochs 0–250): left y-axis (blue, 5-epoch rolling mean) is average moves per self-play game; right y-axis (purple) is epoch time. Dashed lines mark Phase 2 (epoch 16) and Phase 3 (epoch 66). Step increases at phase boundaries are driven by curriculum and MCTS budget changes, as epoch time scales with games × avg_moves × sims/move. At Phase 1→2, moves jump 56.7→80.3 (+42%) and time 1.4→4.7 min (~~3.3×). At Phase 2→3, moves jump 73.7→135.3 (+84%) and time 4.2→14.6 min (~~3.5×). By epoch 250, games average 137 moves and epochs take 16.1 min. The 500k-position replay buffer saturates at epoch 88, marking a stable, high-throughput data regime.
