**AZ-S-4: Policy Entropy.** Shannon entropy (nats) of the **MCTS visit-count policy targets** sampled from the replay buffer (not the network softmax), shown as a 5-epoch centered rolling average with the raw trace faintly behind; dotted line marks the initial value (1.311 nats); dashed verticals mark Phase 2 (epoch 16) and Phase 3 (epoch 66). Entropy drops rapidly in Phase 1 (1.311 → 1.150 by epoch 15) and steps down at the Phase-2 transition (1.131 by epoch 16), then remains in a plateau band through Phase 2 (1.110–1.177). After Phase 3 begins, entropy declines further, reaching a minimum of **0.932** at epoch 134, before gradually increasing again and finishing at **1.015** by epoch 250. The nonzero floor is structurally maintained by Dirichlet root noise (α=1.2, ε=0.35) and high-temperature opening sampling (T=1.0 in early moves), which inject diversity into stored visit counts. The Phase-3 decline and late rebound are consistent with changing target sharpness under higher search budget and with buffer/distribution shifts across game stages, motivating follow-up analysis stratifying entropy by move index (openings vs mid/late game) and reporting decisive/draw fractions over time.
